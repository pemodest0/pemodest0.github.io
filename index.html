About

Theoretical physics with working code

I am a physicist and MSc student at the São Carlos Institute of Physics (University of São Paulo), supported by a national graduate research fellowship. My research is on low-energy quantum scattering in ultracold atomic gases, in close collaboration with an experimental cold-atom laboratory.

Before that I completed a Bachelor's degree in Medical Physics at the University of São Paulo, did undergraduate research in computational neuroscience, and worked as a teaching assistant in Theoretical Mechanics, Statistical Physics and Computational Physics.
